REarth Electrode Solutions

Earthing – Single-family house with external lighting protection Whether existing or new buildings, a functioning earth-termination system is the prerequisite for the safe operation of electric systems in buildings and for protecting people from dangerous high voltages. The design and implementation of the earth-termination system are of central importance.

When implementing the earthing concept, e.g. in the form of a foundation earth electrode, changes are no longer possible after the concrete has set. Omissions or errors during the construction phase cannot be corrected later or, at least, not without a great deal of effort and expense.

Carrying out an earthing study is a mandatory requirement for every new installation project. The purpose of earthing in the electrical network is primarily to ensure safety.

DEHNterra is a fulgurites free, and green backfill earthing solution. The solution is critically synthesized to be compactable with a very good flow percentage value to ease out the installation of the earth pit. DEHNterra has been designed to increase the contact area between earth rod and inner wall of the earth pit.

High grade earthing solution

DEHN Terra provides a least-resistance path for fault and short-circuit currents. This maintenance-free, green earthing solution is soil-compacting, eco-friendly, and ensures long earth-pit life with stable resistivity of 0.02–0.12 Ωm, meeting required standards across diverse soil conditions worldwide reliably safely.

Low Ground Resistance

An important requirement for a perfect earth enhancement material is to maintain a low grounding resistance, with minimal fluctuation for a long period of time. DEHN Terra is a robust solution which is checked and found helpful in reducing and maintaining the earth resistance.
