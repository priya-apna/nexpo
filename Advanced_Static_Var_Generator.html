About ASVG

The Advanced Static Var Generator (ASVG) from Nexpo Power Solutions Pvt. Ltd. is a next-generation power quality solution designed to deliver fast reactive power compensation, harmonic filtering, and three-phase load balancing in a single, intelligent system.

ASVG combines the benefits of Static Var Generators (SVG) and active harmonic filters, making it an ideal solution for industries facing poor power factor, harmonic distortion, and load unbalance issues. It ensures stable, efficient, and reliable operation of electrical systems in both low-voltage and high-voltage applications.

Why It Matters

ASVG is designed for demanding electrical environments where power quality directly affects system efficiency, equipment life, and operational stability.

It helps maintain near-unity power factor, minimizes total harmonic distortion, and supports balanced three-phase current operation for better overall performance.

Key Benefits of Nexpo ASVG

Reactive Power Compensation

  • Maintains power factor close to unity (Cos Ø = 1.00)
  • Instant response to dynamic load changes
  • Improves energy efficiency and reduces electricity penalties

Capacitive & Inductive Compensation

  • Wide compensation range from –1 to +1
  • Suitable for both capacitive and inductive loads
  • Smooth and stable system operation

Harmonic Mitigation

  • Effectively filters major harmonic orders
  • 3rd, 5th, 7th, 9th, and 11th harmonics
  • Reduces Total Harmonic Distortion (THD)
  • Protects transformers, cables, and sensitive equipment

Three-Phase Load Unbalance Correction

  • Corrects current unbalance across all three phases
  • Enhances system stability and equipment lifespan
  • Ideal for industries with uneven load distribution

Flexible & Modular Design

  • Reactive power correction

  • Or a combination of both
  • Compact, modular, and scalable design
  • Cost-effective solution for future expansion

Advanced Working Principle

Nexpo ASVG uses external Current Transformers (CTs) to continuously monitor load current in real time. An advanced DSP controller analyzes reactive power demand and harmonic content, then generates precise PWM control signals for the internal IGBT-based inverter.

By dynamically controlling the phase and amplitude of output current, the ASVG:

  • Absorbs or injects reactive power instantly
  • Cancels harmonic currents accurately
  • Balances three-phase currents effectively

This ensures fast dynamic response, high accuracy, and superior power quality under all operating conditions.
